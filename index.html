Clean Power Everywhere

Peter Lilienthal

My goal in creating the HOMER software was for a simple model that helped people get clean power everywhere. These are even simpler pieces of software.

Projects

  • Solar Battery Simulator This is the flagship model. It was originally conceived to show how cost-effective cooking with PV could be. It can actually be used to look at any application that uses load management instead of a backup generator or grid connection.
  • PV + Battery Estimator This is an earlier version that uses HOMER's optimizer instead of having Claude create a new simulator. It requires the PV+Battery system to provide 24 hour constant power (100% capacity factor) to compete against a nuclear plant. That is unrealistically conservative because actual utility load factors are more like 60% and nuclear plants can't load follow.
  • Temperature Graph This isn't directly about clean power. I've just been marveling that here in Colorado the temperatures have consistently been above average for months and wanted a new way to visualize that. After creating that it was easy to get HOMER to embellish it with other locations and trends.
